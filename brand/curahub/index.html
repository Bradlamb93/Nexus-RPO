01 · Brand foundation

What CuraHub represents

A central operating system for modern care services. Cura comes from the Latin word for care. Hub is the single connected place where care operations, people, residents, families and decisions come together.

Brand promise

Make care operations clearer, more connected and easier to act on.

Personality

Human, capable and quietly confident.

Warm enough for families. Credible enough for operators, commissioners and multi-site care groups.

Who it serves
  • Registered managers and home managers
  • Group operators and regional directors
  • Care teams, nurses and support staff
  • Residents and their families
  • Commissioners and inspectors, indirectly

The name

Write it as CuraHub: capital C, capital H, no space. Never Cura Hub, Curahub or CURAHUB in running text. The company and the platform share the name. Modules take the name as a prefix, for example CuraHub Workforce.

The idea in one line

One source of truth for a care service, with the people around it connected to it. Everything in this pack, from the open C in the logo to the teal reserved for actions, follows from that idea.

04 · Typography

Clear, modern and highly readable

Manrope carries headings, campaigns and high-impact statements. Inter carries the product interface, body text, forms, tables and operational information. Both are open source and free to use in product and print.

Manrope

Semibold 600 and ExtraBold 800

Headings, numbers on dashboards, campaign lines and the wordmark. Tighten tracking slightly above 26 px.

Inter

AaBbCc 0123456789
Regular 400, Medium 500, Semibold 600

Interface, body copy, forms and tables. Turn on tabular numerals wherever figures sit in columns.

Rules

  • Sentence case for headings, buttons and labels. Uppercase only for short eyebrow labels with letter-spacing.
  • Short headings, plain English, no jargon. Running text stays under 70 characters a line.
  • Where brand fonts are unavailable, use Arial or Helvetica. Never substitute a serif or decorative face.

Clarity and action

Naming modules

Always the full form on first mention: CuraHub Workforce. In navigation and inside the product, the short form Workforce is fine because the master brand is already present.

Module marks

The letter tile is a wayfinding device, not a logo. Navy tile, teal letter, Manrope ExtraBold. It never appears with the CuraHub icon at the same size and never on external materials on its own.

Adding a module

A new module needs a one-word name, a two-word value statement and a single letter that is not already taken. It joins this sheet before anything ships.

06 · Voice and tone

Human, clear and quietly confident

Human

Speak about people before processes. Warm does not mean sentimental.

Clear

Plain English, active verbs, short explanations. Make the next action obvious.

Confident

Assured and evidence-led, without exaggeration or empty superlatives.

Operational

Show how the platform improves daily work, oversight and decisions.

Inclusive

Write for residents, relatives, care teams, managers and group operators.

Say this

  • “Three training certificates expire this month. Book renewals now.”
  • “Your rota for next week is published. Two night shifts are still open.”
  • “Margaret enjoyed the garden this afternoon. Here is a photo from the team.”
  • “Occupancy is 94%, up two beds on last month.”

Not this

  • “Leverage our best-in-class compliance engine to revolutionise your outcomes.”
  • “ALERT: CRITICAL ROTA GAP DETECTED.”
  • “Your loved one’s wellbeing journey has been updated in the system.”
  • “Unfortunately an error has occurred. Please try again later.”

07 · Digital principles

Designed around action and trust

Seven rules every screen is checked against before it ships. They apply to the web platform, the mobile app and the family portal equally.

One obvious primary action per screen.

One teal button. Everything else is secondary or tertiary.

Teal for actions and emphasis, not long text.

Links and text use Teal Ink. Paragraphs stay Graphite.

Pair every status colour with a word or icon.

Colour alone is never the signal.

Generous spacing and calm, uncluttered layouts.

Use the 8-point grid. When in doubt, add space.

Make exceptions, deadlines and risks visible without alarm.

Surface what needs attention first, in a measured tone.

Real care settings and genuine human moments in photography.

No stock handshakes, no staged smiles, no stethoscope close-ups.

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA and design accessibility in from the start.

Contrast, focus states, keyboard paths and 44 px touch targets.

The test

Could a registered manager on a night shift understand this screen in five seconds and know what to do next?

What changes

  • Soft Aqua panels replace Cloud for warmth. Deep Navy is used only for the status bar and dialogs.
  • Photos are full-bleed within the card and always real. The caption names who wrote the update.

  • Type scale, spacing tokens, radii and focus states.

11 · Adopting the name

Moving from the working name to CuraHub

The visual system carries over unchanged. The name touches more places than it seems, so work through this list before anything public goes out.

Protect it first

Clearance search at the UK IPO in classes 9, 42 and 44, and check similar marks such as Cura, CuraHub and Cura Systems already trading in UK care software.
File the UK trade mark for the word and the icon once the search is clear.
Companies House name check and change, or a new trading name registered.
Domains: curahub.co.uk and curahub.uk as a minimum, with .com if available. Note that curahub.app is already in use by another product.
Social handles on LinkedIn, X, Instagram and Facebook secured on the same day.

Then switch the product

App names, bundle identifiers and store listings on iOS and Android.
Product name, email sender name and from-address on every automated message.
Login page, favicon, browser title and PDF exports.
In-app help, onboarding copy and release notes.
API names, subdomains and status page.

Then everything else

Proposals, contracts, DPIA and data processing agreements.
Email signatures, letterheads, invoice templates.
Website, pitch deck, one-pagers and case studies.
Existing customers told once, clearly, with the reason and the date.
Old name redirected for 12 months, then retired.
