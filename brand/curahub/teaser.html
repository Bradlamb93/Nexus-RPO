The problem

Care operations run on too many systems that never talk to each other

A typical home juggles a rota tool, a compliance folder, a spreadsheet for occupancy, a payroll export, an agency portal and a family newsletter. Every handover between them is a place where time, money and confidence leak away.

TodayRotas in one app, training records in another, occupancy in a spreadsheet, agency bookings by phone, timesheets on paper, families updated by email when there is time.

What CuraHub offers

Six modules. One platform.

Start with the modules that solve today's problem and switch on the rest as you grow. Every module works from the same resident, staff and shift records.

W

CuraHub Workforce

  • Rotas, attendance and clocking in one view
  • Leave, absence and staff records
  • Agency control with rates and spend tracked per shift
C

CuraHub Compliance

  • CQC readiness with evidence in one place
  • Training, DBS and right-to-work expiry tracking
  • Policies, documents and audits with reminders
O

CuraHub Occupancy

  • Enquiries, assessments and admissions pipeline
  • Resident administration and funding details
  • Occupancy oversight across every home
P

CuraHub Pay

  • Timesheets generated from real attendance
  • Rates, enhancements and billing inputs
  • Payroll preparation without re-keying
I

CuraHub Insights

  • Dashboards for home, region and group
  • Trends and exceptions surfaced automatically
  • Decision-ready reporting for boards and commissioners
F

CuraHub Family

  • Secure updates and photos from the care team
  • Two-way messaging and visit booking
  • Meaningful visibility into a loved one's care

Deliberately not included

Medication management sits outside CuraHub. We integrate with the eMAR you already trust rather than asking you to change it.
